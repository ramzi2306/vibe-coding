# Marketing in the era of Artificial Intelligence: speaker notes

One line per slide to say out loud, then the transition to the next one.

| # | Slide | Say | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Artificial Intelligence | "Tonight I won't show you tools. Tools change every month. I'll show you a way of thinking." | "Let me start with who you will compete against." |
| 2 | 3 people. 30 agents. | "Your next competitor is not a bigger company. They publish every day, test every idea, answer every customer. And they never sleep." | "So the question is not can AI do marketing. It's how far do you let it go." |
| 3 | Three levels of AI | "You ask, it answers. You delegate, it delivers. You design, it runs. Most teams are stuck at level 1 and think they are using AI." | "To see what level 3 changes, look at marketing as it is today." |
| 4 | A headcount game | "More channels meant more people, more hours, more cost. Everyone running, nobody moving." | "Here is the whole map." |
| 5 | Map, AI off | Walk two or three channels. "Every new channel is a new hire. Everything passes through people." | Pause. "Now flip the switch." |
| 6 | Map, AI on | "Same channels. Each one has an agent. And every output becomes another agent's input. That's when it stops being a tool and becomes a system." | "One example in detail." |
| 7 | The machine that feeds itself | "Every question your customers type is tomorrow's article. The machine feeds itself." | "Same pattern everywhere." |
| 8 | The content factory | "Faceless video, AI avatar, media buying. Signal, agent, action, feedback. If you can draw it like this, you can automate it." | "This looks huge. So let's make it small." |
| 9 | Push the first domino | "You don't need the whole map. You need the first domino. One task, one agent, one metric." | "Execution is half the story. The other half is knowing what's happening." |
| 10 | Your analyst never sleeps | "AI is not only your hands. It's your eyes. Reports, concerns, alerts, live." | "What does that look like at 3 in the morning?" |
| 11 | It already fixed it | "Your campaign breaks at 3 a.m. Who notices? Until today, nobody. From now on, the agent, and it has already paused it." (example scenario) | "How do we get there?" |
| 12 | Eyes first, hands later | "An AI without data is a consultant with a blindfold. Give it touchpoints. Read access first, actions later." | "If the machine executes and watches, what's left for us? The most valuable part." |
| 13 | Experience has never been so valuable | "Everyone in this room has the same AI. Nobody has your method. AI gives everybody the average. Your experience is what's above the average." | "The French have a name for it." |
| 14 | Clone your method | "La matérialisation de l'expertise. Take what's in your head and make it executable. Teach it like a junior. Every correction becomes a rule." | "And why do all this?" |
| 15 | Not replaced. Multiplied. | "The goal is not to replace you. It's to multiply you." | "And once the system is stable, something opens." |
| 16 | The next level opens | "A few stable months give you clean history and live data in one place. That's real business intelligence." | "And on top of that." |
| 17 | The cherry on top | "First it executes. Then it watches. Then it understands. Then it predicts." | "Prediction means one thing." |
| 18 | It sees what you can't | "Aide à la décision. It spots opportunities in real time. You still make the call." | "So where do you start?" |
| 19 | One task. One agent. | "Pick one task tonight. Give it to an agent tomorrow morning. Then come tell me what happened." | |

## Power lines

- "A chatbot saves you minutes. A system gives you back your weeks."
- "Operator, then manager, then architect."
- "Don't automate what's impressive. Automate what's repetitive."
- "Give it eyes first, hands later."
- "AI gives everybody the average. Your experience is what's above it."

## Quotes

- John Wanamaker (attributed), for analytics: "Half the money I spend on advertising is wasted; the trouble is I don't know which half."
- Arthur C. Clarke, for the opening: "Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic."
- Alan Kay, for prediction: "The best way to predict the future is to invent it."
- Steve Jobs called the computer "a bicycle for our minds". Follow with: "Agents are the engine on that bicycle."
- W. Edwards Deming (attributed), for data: "Without data, you're just another person with an opinion."

## Notes on the content

- Slide 11 is an invented example (the 38% figure and the times). Say "imagine" when you present it, or swap in a real case of yours.
- Slide 5 and 6 are the same map: go back and forth once, the contrast does the work.
